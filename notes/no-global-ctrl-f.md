# Ctrl+F stays on screens this mod owns

Ctrl+F focuses search on a screen this mod owns. From the world it does nothing: it does not open the inventory, the book, or search.

## The chord is a swap from the world

Sprint defaults to Left Control, and swap-offhand defaults to F. From the world, Ctrl+F is the same press as swapping hands while sprinting. A sprinting player still has to be able to press F. A Control or Shift chord clashes with sprint or sneak the same way. A free letter such as B, or Alt+F, would leave those keys alone.

## Standing still does not fix it

Checking whether the player is sprinting is unreliable. A double-tap forward starts a sprint with Control up. Toggle sprint leaves the sprint binding on after the key is released. The sprint key can be rebound. A player can also hold Control and press F before a sprint has started.

The movement keys are sturdier: the forward, back, left, and right bindings, not the W key. While any of those is down, F still swaps. Ctrl+F would open search only when the player is stopped. That still steals a swap. People swap hands while standing still, often with a finger already on Control, and that press would open the inventory.

## Discovery

Players learn keys from the controls menu. A chord that does nothing while they are moving feels broken. Once they have stopped, they already press E, and Ctrl+F on that screen focuses search. A world chord would only skip that E, and only after they had stopped.
